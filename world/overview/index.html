Overview

Your briefing of the TIR world.

Categories: Lore

A brief overview of the world to get you a most basic idea of what this paracosm entails! There are plenty of links in this file, so you can click on them when you see a topic you're interested in, but you don't have to because you can know everything important from this file!

The world

So, there is a continent that as far as we're concerned is the entire world.

Their technology level is mostly the same as ours (except for maybe a time machine or two).

In the past, the entire continent was just one big Bauhinian Empire, but later, people started getting independent. Five countries split out of the Empire, and the original Empire also underwent its own revolution to become a republic. Learn more in history!

So, there are six countries now. From south to north, they are Bauhinia, Fanton, Atlasia, Westavia (Atlasia and Westavia are next to each other), Moreland, and Sylvandia.

They're locked in an perpetual cold war between Bauhinia and Moreland, splitting the continent into the north and the south.

Both of these two countries have two allies, one staunch ally and one unwilling ally.

  • Bauhinia has Atlasia and Fanton.
  • Moreland has Westavia and Sylvandia.

The cold war is the most important thing about the world! It is the backdrop against which all stories are played out. I'm a huge spy nerd if you couldn't tell. If you take away just one thing, just remember that Bauhinia and Moreland are politically opposed, and their allies are the two countries closest to them!

Oh by the way the world of TIR is flat. This is canon. See more in Geography.

The people

Everyone belongs to a race and has a soul. Some people have superpowers, but they're not that relevant most of the time. Souls are the most important concept here. You see how every character has a certain color associated with them? That's their soul color!

And although it's a heavy work in progress, know that everyone on the continent speaks the same language. This is because of the Bauhinian Empire.

Major storylines

I love it when many characters' lives intertwine and everyone is the protagonist of their own story but side characters in someone else's story, so that's basically TIR's structure too. There's no clear protagonist aside from Sparky, and no clear main storyline. That being said, here are some of the major storylines you can follow!

  • Sparky, the center of our universe. She solves mysteries and gets in trouble with her friends. Also her stepdad Peacock is the personal attorney of chairman Hyrax. Later, after Sparky grew up, Hyrax had his son Animus replace him as the chairman, and had Sparky become Animus's bodyguard. See her stories here.
  • Kismet, the dictator president of Bauhinia, has his own arc and all.
  • Candlelight, a pile of misery, goes through a lot of shit.
  • Sunflower, a girl who paracosms, dies.
  • Qibli, one of Sparky's friends, becomes an outlaw and stuff.
  • Sky, one of Sparky's friends, becomes a spymaster and stuff and then goes to teach at Spy School.
  • Harmony and Sugarcane's friendship gets tested as they go to said spy school.

Of course there are many more characters and their arcs, but that's about it for the overview!
