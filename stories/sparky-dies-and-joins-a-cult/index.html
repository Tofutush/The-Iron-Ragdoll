Sparky Dies and Joins a Cult

Sparky "dies," but gets revived thanks to our great and powerful and magical leader.

Main Cast
Sparky Animus
Time period
somewhere around 2047
Complete
Tags
Bauhinia, Complete, Oneshot

Sparky was texting and driving when she drove off a bend and crashed. (This was somewhere in Bauhinia, but not Coastline.)

She woke up wearing some creepy white funeral clothes and a couple people standing over her. It was weirdly dark. The room was lit only with candles. She asked what happened.

One of the men told her she had died. Many people, he said, die from crashing off that bend. Locals call it the Bend of Hell.

Aw, fuck, Sparky said. Is this the afterlife, then? Are you like, God or something?

Ha, no way, the man said. You are still in the mortal realm. You see, I have a certain capability for magic. And my special magical talent is that I can revive people.

Wow! Sparky was impressed. One of my friends was the greatest magician to ever have lived and even she couldn't revive people!

Unfortunately, the man continued, my powers are not all that powerful. Now you cannot stray too far away from here, the place you were revived, or you turn back into a corpse. And you cannot breathe fresh air and cannot see sunlight. Candlelight is the only safe light source for you.

What a weird set of restrictions, Sparky said, but I'll take that over dying any day!

That's the spirit! The man led Sparky to a room full of plants. Now to ensure that I can feed all of you, you need to help me take care of these plants, trim their leaves, and package them into those little cigarettes.

That's a totally normal and reasonable thing to do, Sparky said. But also can't I get a laptop and try to do some remote work?

Um, ah, the man said, you're also allergic to the Internet. More than a few minutes' of Internet use can render you back dead.

Wow that sucks, Sparky said, but okay.

Also call me "my leader" because without me you'd be dead.

Okay, my leader! Sparky said.

So the days went by. Sparky and the other dead-but-revived people trimmed leaves, grew more plants, and wrote letters for their leader to send home, except that they always went straight into the trash.

Why hasn't my husband written back? Sparky would ask.

Mailmen tend to die in the Bend of Hell, their leader would reply.

Can't you revive them too?

I can't revive too many people in rapid succession.

Complete reasonable, Sparky nodded.

Until one day Animus and the others finally found them. He beat the shit out of anyone who tried to stop him, beat some sense into Sparky, and drove her back to Coastline. She screamed when he dragged her out into Nature, Sunlight, and Fresh Air, but then realized she wasn't dying. Also the Bend of Hell was soon reinforced to minimize future casualties, and the cult leader's weed business was completely busted and Animus took all the seized money for himself.

Don't text and drive, people.
