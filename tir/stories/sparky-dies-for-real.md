Sparky was sitting in Animus' home at the roof of the Starcorp skyscraper, drinking. It was a Blood Moon and she climbed on the railing, wanting to pluck the moon out of the sky. She fell down due to imbalance from intoxication. See ya sucka.

Yeah that's the entire thing. Animus came up too late. Peacock saw her falling from a random window inside the building. Nightsun bolted the fuck away from Bauhinia.

She died and went to [Meng Po](/world/meng-po/) where she spat out her soup and reincarnated as [Peacock](/characters/peacock/) with her memories retained.

To be fair, it was one of the reddest Blood Moons in *years*. I don't blame her. Do you?
