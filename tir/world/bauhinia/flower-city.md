Capital of [Bauhinia](/world/bauhinia/), near the geographical center of the country. The flower, of course, means the bauhinia flower.

## History

Flower City used to be the capital of the [Bauhinian Empire](/world/bauhinian-empire/) too. After the revolution, the republicans redecorated the emperor's palace and simply used that as the government building.

## Geography

There's an Inner city, the OG. The Outer city was developed in modern times, and sit outside of the ancient city wall.
