Ah, the Bauhinian Empire. It had been around for more than three thousand years before the industrial revolution broke it all down (see: [history](/world/history/)).

Its capital is situated in [Flower City](/world/bauhinia/flower-city/), but the Emperor also has seasonal palaces: the Winter Palace in [Froggy Island](/world/bauhinia/froggy-island/), and the Summer Palace somewhere in [Sylvandia](/world/sylvandia/) (I haven't decided where exactly yet).

## Creation

The formation of the Empire dates too far back to have any objective accounts. The consensus among modern historians is that the Empire was created by an ancient tribe called the Bauhinia Tribe, who used to be nomadic, roaming around the Atlasian region, before they settled down and shifted towards agriculture.

What we know for sure is that any account detailing either [God](/world/mythology/#god) or [Copy](/world/mythology/#copy) creating the Empire and blessing the Emperor is mere mythology, as the characters of God and Copy themselves were conceptualized long after the creation of the Empire.

## Succession

There's a bit of history surrounding the process of choosing the next heir. In the early days of the Empire, Emperors were chosen by merit. Whoever has the most skills to govern will become the next Emperor.

One day, an Emperor decided to choose his oldest son to succeed him. This caused skepticism, especially when there were more suitable candidates who weren't a literal child. The Emperor pulled a stamp out of his ass and called it the Royal Seal, saying that this validates his choice. He gave the Seal to his son. As long as he has it, he is the heir. It was around that time that legends surrounding God started to develop, too.

Later down the line, one Emperor decided that even setting the heir with the Seal wasn't enough, because that decision is permanent — still subject to checks from his oldest son, who possesses the Seal. Instead, he kept the Seal by him all his life, and noted the heir in a little piece of paper tucked inside his pillow. This way, he could change the heir any time he wanted to. This was when the idea of the Seal as a channel through which to exert God's authority started. (One time the Emperor and his heir died together in an accident and sparked quite the succession drama. After that they started doing 2nd and 3rd choice candidates.)

## Bauhinia, the color

An unworldly color of bright pink, it was made with bauhinia flowers and a lot of expert technique. It is used most famously for the stamping of the Royal Seal. Only Emperors can use this color. It is #F01ACE in case you're wondering.

## The Close Guards

The Emperor's personal security force. They created [Earth-style](/world/martial-arts/#earth-style) martial arts, in ancient times when most technologies we know and love didn't exist; and its techniques, successful enough to keep them safe for centuries, were kept extremely secret. Training was passed down from senior members to new recruits, and trainees who failed to make it onto the team were executed to ensure they didn't spread what they learned to those who shouldn't hear it. None who made it their mission to learn by studying the Guards had stayed alive long enough to learn anything.

During the Bauhinian revolution, most of them defended the emperor to the death, save for this one guy who was an undercover Republican. He helped the revolutionaries kill the emperor, and for years he was remembered as the only person on the continent who even knew Earth-style martial arts, seeing as he personally ensured the death of many members of his team. Fearing that this art form would die out forever (and also everyone was yelling at him to do it), he trained many people in Earth-style martial arts, so that it survived and flourished to this day.

## Famous figures

### Emperor Lilypad

One of the most famous and influential Emperors in history who brought unprecedented prosperity to the Empire, Lilypad was the one who moved the Empire's capital to Flower City and ordered the construction of the [Bauhinia Tower](/world/bauhinia/bauhinia-tower/).

### Emperor Lotus

The one that created the Close Guards and whatever the fuck the ancient equivalent of the MSS is.

### Emperor Lily

The last emperor before the end of the Empire; intentionally yet ironically named after Lilypad. [Orchid](/characters/orchid/) and [Peony](/characters/peony/)'s great-great grandfather.
