## Geography

Sylvandia borders [Moreland](/world/moreland/) on the south, and shares a short border with [Atlasia](/world/atlasia/) on the southeast.

The closest geographical mirror to Sylvandia is Siberia.

## History

Rebellions in the north of the Bauhinian Empire are like trees in a forest, though in the past, they have always been easy to crush and dealt no real damage to the rulers in the south. But since the industrial revolution and Bauhinia's own republican revolution, the new technology and political unrest made independence movements pop up like bamboo sprouts after rain. Though the central problem present all throughout history persisted — the movements are scattered and ununited.

With help from the Republican Revolution in Bauhinia, five different states declared their independence and set up republican governance: [Solarine](/world/sylvandia/solarine/) and [Lunarale](/world/sylvandia/lunarale/) in 1948, [Holliloli](/world/sylvandia/holliloli/) in 1949, [Snowdale](/world/sylvandia/snowdale/) in 1950, and [Mistle](/world/mistle/) in 1951. That basically got most of the continent covered. Shortly after the creation of Mistle, on July 29<sup>th</sup>, Moreland urged the states to form a federal government with the capital set in Bonnie, a city on the border of Holliloli and Snowdale. To this day, Sylvandia still sees vast Bauhinian influence. Resisting them often leads to sanctions and measures used to worsen living conditions there, and sometimes people do wonder why they don't just cave in to Bauhinia and enjoy the perks of being their ally when Moreland doesn't give them that much help anyways, but the truth was that ultimately neither Bauhinia and Moreland has Sylvandia's best interests at heart and the only thing that can benefit all Sylvandians in the long run was true Sylvandian independence, which Moreland is willing to give and Bauhinia wants to take away.
