"Where *were* you, papa?" Jelly pouted.

"I told you, I had to make sure the event runs smoothly. Checking in with staff, talking to people," Checkers said as he led us back into the elevator.

"About *what*?" Jelly went to the back side and squatted down in the corner.

"*Work*, honey. We've been over this."

Sugarcane gave me a meaningful glance, which I caught. What sort of work?

"What *sort* of work?" Jelly echoed our question out loud.

"Trying to stop Auntie Moonie from giving us all diabetes, for example," Checkers said. "I already let you invite your friends. Why not spend the evening with them?"

"I already am doing it," Jelly said, "before you came here to chase us out."

"It's more fun outside! You know, where the actual event is? Your friends missed the dance performance."

"We *were* watching it," Jelly said.

Sugarcane and I stood awkwardly against the elevator wall. The door opened to the first floor and we stepped out. Checkers held the door open while Jelly moved out at a snail's pace.

"Fine," she said. "I'll stay out of your nose."

"That is appreciated," Checkers said.

We had arrived at the door again. Out through it and we would be back to the festivities, separated again from whatever Checkers was doing behind the locked doors of this imposing and secretive building. But Checkers did not open it just yet.

"Spotty? I have something for you," he said.

"Oh?" Sugarcane sounded genuinely surprised.

Checkers pulled out a small tube from his pocket. "Anti-itch cream. Put that on your head. Can't have you scratching it."

"Thanks, uncle, but I don't scratch it."

"You will in your sleep. Doesn't it feel itchy?"

"It does occasionally," Sugarcane admitted.

"Then take this. Better apply some right now."

"Thank you, uncle," Sugarcane took the tube.

Checkers opened the door and the music and lights leaked in. "Out, kids!" he said as he shoved us out the door and closed it behind us.

Jelly stood in the doorway in silence for a moment. Then she said, "I'm sorry."

"Don't be," I said. "Let's go and make sure Spotty gets the anti-itch cream on her head."

"But I want to wash my hair today!" Sugarcane said. "I'm just gonna wash it right off. I'll do it before bed."

"Goober boogers," Jelly said, pinching her nose in the iconic gesture.

"Come on!" Sugarcane smacked her playfully on the shoulder. "You're doing this *too*?"

"I think Auntie Moonie actually bought some. Wanna check it out?"

"Can't believe I'm doing this," Sugarcane said. "But why not."

The Goober Booger gummies were apparently quite popular with the kids. We managed to hoard the last bits for ourselves. They tasted a bit different from last time; probably a different brand. This one was chewier.

"I hate it when dads do that," Sugarcane suddenly said.

"No, no, I should apologize," Jelly said. "I should've focused more on playing with you than on looking for papa."
