We jogged along the side of the building, on a little path surrounded by hedges, until we came to a big yard with a stage and rows of folding chairs sitting attentively. To the side was a long table with all sorts of snacks, still being laid out. A lot of adults, some with children, were scattered around the place. Some turned their heads over to us, but quickly lost interest when they saw that it was just Jelly and her friends.

At the snacks table, Jelly grabbed a handful of cookies, pocketed half of it, and handed the other half to us. "These are really good!" she said as she immediately reached for another basket. "Rose pies!" She took two for herself and handed one to us each. "Try it, try it!"

Sugarcane gave me a wry smile. *Rose* pies; get it?

I was more focused on things other than our childhood tormentor, though. "What do you mean, *rose* pies?"

"It's… rose pie. Like pie, with roses. A rose pie." Jelly said with her mouth full.

"But… Is it like, rose… flavored? Or does it actually have roses in it?"

Jelly made a funny face as she swallowed what was in her mouth as quickly as possible. Before I could tell her to take it easy, she succeeded in her gluttony and said, "Auntie Moonie made these herself! Of course it has real roses in it!"

"You can *eat* roses?" I gawked at the unsuspecting pie in my hand. It was really small, and was starting to fall apart as I held onto it for too long.

"Well, yeah," Jelly said. "You've never tried one before? That's horrible! Try it!"

Sugarcane took a bit first. "It's really sweet!" she said.

"Papa hates how Auntie Moonie always puts too much sugar in things," Jelly chuckled. "But I like it."

Tentatively, I also took a bite. The outside of the pie was very flaky, and I felt like I was eating paper. The inside was indeed a sort of sickly sweetness of ground-up rose petals soaked in sugar.

Jelly had finished her second pie and was already moving on. She grabbed three plastic cups from a stack and started filling them up from a teapot. She handed the first full cup to me. "Milk tea!"

"Thanks," I said as I took a sip, but immediately retched. "This is so salty!"

"Oh, yeah," Jelly said. "Papa made this and he didn't let Auntie Moonie touch it a single bit. You don't like it?"

"It would take some getting used to," I said.

Jelly handed another full cup to Sugarcane, who took the chance to grab the teapot and fill Jelly's own cup, which Jelly downed in one gulp.

"Jelly, dear, leave some for the guests," Checkers suddenly materialized behind us.

"Make more, then," Jelly stuck out her tongue.

"Come on, I can make this for you any day. Today this is for the guests." Checkers lifted the teapot to get a feel of how much was left. He frowned. "Let me go refill this."

"Do you need help with that, uncle?" Sugarcane immediately said.

"I can handle that, thanks."

After Checkers left, also lifted her cup to take a sip. "This is salty indeed," she frowned.

"This is how milk tea is supposed to taste," Jelly said. "No one makes it better than Papa."

I stood there awkwardly, holding the cup, still nearly full, in my hand. Jelly seemed intent on waiting for her dad to come back.

"So are we just waiting for him?" Sugarcane asked.

"Um-hm," Jelly nodded. "He'll be back soon. See, there he is!"

She rushed forward to take the full teapot from Checkers' hand, but he pulled it away. "This is too heavy for you."

"No it isn't."

"Yes it is," Checkers set the pot on the table. Jelly went to lift it up again to prove her point. Checkers interpreted it as her wanting to drink more tea, and 



"Awwww, papa…" Jelly tried to protest. Checkers set the teapot down and left with Moonie.

"Papa, auntie, where are you going?" Jelly called after her.

"Your papa and I have a lot of work to do to make sure the event runs smoothly," Moonie said. "Go make your friends feel at home, won't you?"

"I will!" Jelly yelled. Checkers and Moonie disappeared into the building.
