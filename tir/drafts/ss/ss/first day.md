The night before school started, I nearly couldn't fall asleep. I kept tossing and turning as excitement overwhelmed me. I wished that time would go quicker and it could be morning soon, but then I realized that actually falling asleep was the best way to skip time quickly, so I brought all my attention onto trying to fall asleep, which seemed to have the opposite effect. I couldn't remember when I finally drifted into unconsciousness, but I woke with a start when my alarm rang. Six thirty, like I had set it.
