It was a small playground in a tiny park about halfway between my home and Sugarcane's. When I got there, she was sitting on her favorite bench.

She saw me and waved. I waved back, trotted over, and plopped down beside her.

"Sugarcane!" I didn't waste time catching my breath. "Did you get recruited into Ministry Affiliate?"

"Um, what?"

"Ministry Affiliate! A teacher there came to my home this morning and asked me to go there. He said you already agreed!"

"Harmony!" Sugarcane looked a bit serious. "He said not to tell anyone else."

"Does it matter, really?" I shrugged. "I mean, if we're both attending, why hide it from each other?"

Sugarcane considered my words for a moment, and said, "well, if he told you about it first, I guess that makes it okay. Yes, I have made my choice to attend."

"This is amazing!" I wrapped Sugarcane into a hug.

"You still need to keep your grades up, though," Sugarcane warned me. "Or they'll kick you out."

"I totally got this," I said. "Just help me cheat."

"Harmony!" Sugarcane stomped her foot.

"I'm just kidding! Come on, my grades are gonna be fine and I've got more pressing questions. What are we going to do at Ministry Affiliate? Would we get sent on *dangerous missions*? Catch *spies*? Do we get to *save the world*?"

"I'm sure we're just gonna be reading textbooks and taking exams," Sugarcane shrugged. "My uncle always said that he caught spies with paperwork, not epic car-chases."

"All I mean is, I'm *excited*. Aren't you?"

"I am," Sugarcane smiled. "I've always wanted to go to that school. I want to do whatever my uncle is doing. I guess I'm just nervous now that I really got the chance."

"That's fair," I said. "Wanna come over for dinner?"

"My parents already made dinner, but thanks."

We waved each other goodbye, and I headed home.
