# Core Meanings Checklist

## by kvk / good-mcrn-ing

This list is meant to guide the early development of a general-purpose communication system. It is based on experience with minimal constructed languages on one hand, and partial or unfinished logographies on the other. It can act as an unbalanced pass-fail test: a language that can convey every entry may be fit to serve a population in daily life; a language that cannot is almost certainly too limiting. Users are assumed to be modern humans, though in-world speakers in fiction may not be.

Note that this list is not a template for a lexicon. The translation for an entry may be a dedicated lexeme, reused lexeme from another context, transparent phrase, grammar rule, or any other mechanism so long as it conveys the intended meaning. Part of speech is ignored: an entry like ‘person’ is satisfied by ‘X be a person’, ‘which is a person’, ‘X interact with Y as a person’, and many others.

The categories below are loose and placed in a random order. Comma-separated elements within an entry are examples selected from a broader unified semantic range. X, Y, Z stand for entities. P, Q, R stand for propositions.

## Structure

* modification of noun by clause: X about which it is true that P, X which P  
* modification of noun by noun: X related to Y, X of Y, X identifiable by knowing about Y  
* syntactic noun derived from clause: X be act or phenomenon where P

## Ontology

* person, sapient being, creature of the same type as the speakers  
* object, item, thing (tangible inanimate)  
* concept, construct, abstraction (intangible)  
* identity, X be synonymous with Y  
* type, category, kind, X be a type of Y  
* category membership, X be one of Y, X be element of Y  
* create, make, X cause Y to become identifiable thing, X reconfigure something to form Y  
* cause, initiate, X make it so P  
* part, section, X be (physical) component of Y  
* trait, property, X be (abstract) descriptor of Y  
* resemblance, similarity, X be like Y  
* difference, distinction, X be unlike Y  
* do, perform, X engage in Y (Y abstract)  
* means, instrument, method, P by means of X

## Context

* I, speaker  
* you, listener  
* this one, this X, uniquely relevant identifiable X  
* other one, a different X, X in contrast to the one mentioned  
* what thing, what X, X whose identity is requested information

## Logic

* false, negation, it not be the case that P  
* change, begin, it start to be the case that P  
* ability, possibility, it be possible that P  
* coordination, X and Y, all of X and Y and Z  
* implication, if P then Q  
* counterfactual, alternative world, if it were the case that P then it would be the case that Q  
* query, P whose truth value is requested information

## Space

* place, location (feature of world or background)  
* location, position (feature of entity), X be at Y  
* motion, travel, X change location  
* direction, X be in direction Y of origin Z  
* surroundings, vicinity, X be near Y  
* distance, separation, X be far from Y  
* centre, core, X be middle of Y  
* surface, boundary, X be edge of Y  
* ground, terrain, X support Y against gravity  
* up, high, X be top part of Y  
* down, low, X be bottom part of Y  
* positive direction-term in dimension 1  
* negative direction-term in dimension 1  
* positive direction-term in dimension 2  
* negative direction-term in dimension 2

Direction-terms as a group are satisfied by {left, right, forward, back} or {north, south, east, west} or any other set of terms that enable arbitrary navigation in two dimensions.

## Time

* time (instant or period)  
* repeat, P again, X engage in P again  
* habit, P routinely, P reliably  
* take time, X have a duration, X occupy a time-slot  
* past time  
* now, current time  
* future time  
* X exist before Y, P before Q  
* X exist after Y, P after Q

## Quantity

* there exist X, X be part of world  
* none of, there be no X, X be absent  
* one of, single, unique  
* two, pair of  
* several, small positive number of  
* many, large number of  
* all of, entire group, every X  
* group, set, X belong to collection Y  
* much X (X treated as uncountable)  
* little X (X treated as uncountable)

## Mind

* mind, psyche, brain, information processing subsystem and/or its functions  
* communicate, X form messages, X use language  
* think, imagine, X manipulate internal representations  
* signify, indicate, X mean Y, Y be content of X  
* know, X be aware that P  
* perceive, detect, X sense Y  
* remember, memory, X store and recall internal representation of Y  
* feeling, sensation, state of mind, X experience Y  
* want, desire, X be attracted to Y  
* intention, purpose, P in order that Q  
* control, direct, X exert will over Y  
* search, X look for Y  
* good, benefit, X help Y  
* bad, harm, X hurt Y

## Life

* life, X be alive  
* death, X be dead  
* lifeform, organism  
* consume, metabolise, X feed on Y  
* produce, emit, X excrete Y  
* rest, X be idle  
* body, physical form of X  
* injury, X be damaged  
* illness, X be dysfunctional  
* breed, reproduce, X be offspring of Y

## Physics

* substance, material  
* solid matter  
* liquid  
* gas  
* shape, configuration, X be shaped like Y  
* attachment, connection, X be physically linked to Y  
* one-dimensional shape, X be long  
* two-dimensional shape, X be flat  
* hole, cavity, absence of matter  
* physically large  
* physically small  
* contact, X touch Y  
* flex, bend, X be soft  
* turn, rotate, X spin around Y  
* sound, vibration, X make noise  
* light, radiation, X emit light  
* heat, X be warm

## Discourse

* I wish to communicate: “hello”  
* I acknowledge what you said: “okay”  
* I need you to acknowledge: “okay?”  
* I am busy forming a reply: “um…”  
* I need you to repeat: “huh?”  
* I am ready to receive: “go on”  
* I retract my message: “never mind that”  
* I wish to send a long message: “so…”  
* I am ending this conversation: “goodbye”
