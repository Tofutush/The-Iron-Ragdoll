# pre

"Eagle is dead," the officer said.

"Who?"

"Your cousin, Eagle," he stared at me solemnly. "The Westavian operation? You were put down as next-of-kin and he's dead now."

The officer had called me to his office this morning for a briefing. This wasn't what I had expected. I didn't even remember agreeing to be Eagle's cousin.

"Oh," was all I said.

"We need you to go to Westavia now to collect his belongings," the officer said. "And his corpse, but especially his wallet. You can throw away the suitcase if it's mostly only clothes. Bring his wallet. You're leaving tomorrow."

"Wait, on such short notice?"

"He died without informing us prior," the officer snapped. "Here's your passport and documents. Give them a read tonight and get packing quick."

"Shouldn't I have a bit more briefing first?" I asked. Westavia was not a safe place to go.

"You are Eagle's cousin and your name is Kitten. Eagle died of an accident in People's Vanguard, Westavia. You weren't close and you didn't know what the hell your cousin was doing in Westavia, but nevertheless you were notified by the Bauhinian Embassy as Eagle put you down as his next-of-kin. So now you need to head over and deal with it."

"That's it?" I asked.

"It's a simple mission, and it's not like it's your first time in the field, is it?"

"It's my first time going to Westavia. I haven't even gone as a tourist before."

"Ah, all the more reasons to go. Don't be worried. It's not like you're going to die."

A moment of awkward silence.

"Okay," the officer continued, "I see the irony, but really. As far as Westavia's concerned you really are just a cousin. Look both sides before crossing the road. You'll be fine."

"Okay," I said, still not fully convinced. "What was Eagle doing, anyway? What happened to him?"

"Sorry, but you're not on the whitelist," the officer said. "You've known all you need to know."
